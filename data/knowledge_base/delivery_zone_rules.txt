URBAN ZONE TRANSIT AND ACCESS REGULATIONS (MUNICIPAL DIRECTIVE 2026)
1. ZONE A (CENTRAL BUSINESS DISTRICT & RESIDENTIAL)
   - Zero-emission / EV preference enforced between 09:00 and 19:00.
   - Heavy commercial trucks (> 150 kg payload) restricted from narrow residential avenues.
   - Maximum speed limit: 30 km/h.
2. ZONE B (TECH CORRIDOR & COMMERCIAL MALLS)
   - Heavy cargo freight exceeding 50.0 kg prohibited between 17:00 and 20:00 (peak congestion window).
   - Commercial loading bay permit required for vehicles larger than standard vans.
3. ZONE C (UNIVERSITY & CIVIC HUB)
   - Low-noise electric delivery vehicles permitted 24/7.
   - Dual security clearance verification required for institutional deliveries.
4. ZONE D (AIRPORT CARGO & LOGISTICS PARK)
   - 24-hour truck transit permitted.
   - Heavy rainfall protocols: automatic 15 km/h speed reduction during precipitation exceeding 10 mm.
