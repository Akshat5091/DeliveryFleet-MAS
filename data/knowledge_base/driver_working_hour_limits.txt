LABOR SAFETY & DRIVER FATIGUE DIRECTIVE
Section 4.1: Hard Shift Limits
- Max allowable shift duration: 9.0 hours per 24-hour window.
- If a driver reaches 8.5 hours, the dispatch agent must reject any assignment that has an estimated duration exceeding 30 minutes.
- If hours_worked >= 9.0 hours, driver status is immediately marked OFF_DUTY.
Section 4.2: Exception Overrides
- Even under emergency or critical rerouting, legal driver fatigue limits cannot be overridden by automated agents.
- Violations of Section 4.1 are intercepted and rejected by the Business Guardrail check with code SAFETY_HOURS_VIOLATION.
